            How the mix of industries has shifted · weekly

            Band height is how many of this phrase's articles came from each industry that week. Bands stack, so the outline is total coverage and the slices are who was writing it. A phrase changing hands looks like one band thinning while another swells — sequence, not cause: nobody knows whether one industry took the phrase from another or both responded to the same thing.

            Share of each industry's SEC filings mentioning it · since 2016

            Ten years of the same question the map above asks over ten weeks, from company disclosures rather than press coverage. Industry labels are the SEC's own. Quarterly and formal, so it lags events — a company writes about a constraint after the trade press has covered it.
